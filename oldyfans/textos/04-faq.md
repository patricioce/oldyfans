**Where do my shirts come from?**
We're a small US brand run by a Gen Xer who turned fifty and refused to act like it. Every shirt is printed to order at our partners in the US, and international orders are printed close to you so they arrive faster.

**How long until I get it?**
Printing takes 2–5 business days, then USPS delivers in 2–5. Most US orders arrive in 4–10 business days. International orders take 6–20 business days depending on customs.

**How much is shipping?**
Free in the US on 2 or more shirts. Buy 3 and you also save $10. One shirt in the US ships for [COSTO_1_REMERA_US]. International rates show at checkout before you pay.

**What size should I get?**
They're a relaxed unisex fit, true to size. If you're between sizes or like it roomy, size up. Check the size guide on each product page (measurements in inches). Still unsure? Email us and a real person will help.

**What are they made of?**
100% ring-spun cotton, 4.5 oz, pre-shrunk. Light and soft, more "favorite old concert tee" than heavy workwear.

**How do I wash it so the print lasts?**
Turn it inside out, wash cold, tumble dry low or hang dry. Don't iron over the print and skip the bleach. That keeps the design from cracking.

**What if something's wrong?**
Damaged, misprinted or wrong item? We'll replace it or refund you, no need to send it back. [Versión A: Wrong size? We'll reprint it in another size once, free.] You have 30 days from delivery.

**Will I get a tracking number?**
Yes. You'll get it by email as soon as your order ships.

**How do I reach you?**
Email **info@oldyfans.store**. We answer within 1 business day.
