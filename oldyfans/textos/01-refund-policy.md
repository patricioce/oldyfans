We want you to love your shirt. If something isn't right, we'll fix it.

## Damaged, defective or wrong item
If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, defective, or isn't what you ordered, we'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund. You don't need to send the item back.

## Wrong size?
Every shirt is printed to order just for you, so we can't take back shirts for a change of mind. If the size doesn't fit, we'll reprint it in a different size for free the first time (one exchange per order). Just email us a photo of the shirt with the tag visible.

## How to ask
Email **info@oldyfans.store** within **30 days of delivery** with your order number and a photo. A real person answers within 1 business day.

## Refunds
Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## Updates
We may update this policy. The version on this page is the one that applies to your order.
