## When will it arrive?
Every shirt is printed to order, so delivery = printing + shipping.

| | Printing | Shipping | Total (typical) |
|---|---|---|---|
| United States | 2–5 business days | 2–5 business days (USPS) | 4–10 business days |
| International | 2–5 business days | 4–15 business days | 6–20 business days |

International times depend on customs and aren't guaranteed. Import duties or taxes, if any, are paid by the customer.
