30-Day Refund & Size Replacement Policy

Refunds and size replacements are available within 30 days of your original purchase date. We cannot offer either after that window. A replacement does not restart or extend it.

Don’t love it? We’ll refund you.

If you don’t love your 80s Survivors Club tee for any reason, we’ll refund you. No return needed—please donate it or pass it on to a friend who’s earned it.

T-shirt doesn’t fit? We’ll send a new size.

Tell us the size you need (XS to 4XL), and we’ll print and send you a replacement T-shirt with the same design in that size. You don’t need to send the original back.
