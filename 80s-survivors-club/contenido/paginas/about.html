Remember when a stack of bikes in the driveway meant the whole crew was there? When basements became headquarters and arcades were where you spent your allowance? When you could disappear on your bike all day and nobody needed to know where you were?

We do. And we're not apologizing for it.

I'm a Gen X guy who turned fifty, and I made 80s Survivors Club to celebrate the greatest years of my life — the seventies, eighties, and nineties. That era shaped who we became: adventurous, resilient, and still laughing at ourselves without needing permission.

This isn't nostalgia for nostalgia's sake. It's a love letter to the people we were, the friends we had, the freedom we knew, and the kind of childhood that taught us to figure things out on our own. To Atari marathons. To mixtapes and big wheels. To concerts, late nights, and the kind of bond that only happens when you're actually present.

We lived it. We remember it. And we're still here, still adventuring, still traveling, still gathering with friends and family and refusing to stop living fully.

This is for us. Welcome home.
